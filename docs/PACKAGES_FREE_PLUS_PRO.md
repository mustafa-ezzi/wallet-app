# WalletTrails packages

Free, Plus, and Pro. Each paid plan includes everything in the plan below it.

These are the features in the app today. The caps are the package rules. The server does not enforce every cap yet.

Nothing in Trai is saved until the user confirms it.

---

## Free — follow every rupee

A real tracker, with small limits.

- Account with email or Google, and password reset
- Unlimited income, expense, and transfer, including the calculator
- Privacy lock: hide amounts, unlock with biometrics or a PIN, block screenshots
- Offline entries on the phone
- Bill and due reminders
- **2** wallets (bank, cash, or credit card)
- **2** active income sources (salary, contract, one-time, or installments)
- **3** active bills, loans, or amounts owed to you, combined
- **1** budget per month
- About **10** custom categories
- Reports and a basic forecast for this month and **1** previous month
- **1** household, up to **3** members, one ongoing ledger
- **1** person for lend and borrow
- Paste or share a bank message, about **15** approvals in total
- Default theme
- Help & Support

Not in Free: a third wallet, extra themes, the home-screen widget, CSV or PDF, event ledgers, Trai, the always-on bank inbox, Travel Mode.

---

## Plus — household and clarity

Everything in Free, plus:

- Unlimited wallets, income sources, bills, budgets, and categories
- Copy last month’s budgets
- Full report history
- CSV export for personal and household reports
- Household events, closing an event, more members, shared pot and split
- Unlimited people links
- All themes
- Home-screen balance widget
- Trai by typing, about **30** messages a day, on the phone and on the web

Not in Plus: Trai’s microphone, “Hey Trai”, the always-on bank inbox, Travel Mode, PDF export.

---

## Pro — automation

Everything in Plus, plus:

- Trai by voice, spoken “yes, save it”, and hands-free follow-ups
- “Hey Trai” while the Android app is open (not on the lock screen, and not on the web)
- On the web: type, or tap the mic once. No wake word
- About **120** Trai messages a day
- Always-on bank SMS and notification inbox on Android, where the phone allows it
- A generous cap on approved bank messages
- Travel Mode (foreign amount, home-currency books)
- PDF export
