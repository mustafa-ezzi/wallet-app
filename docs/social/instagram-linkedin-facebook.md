**About (paste this):**

```
WalletTrails is a money app for people who want one clear picture of everyday rupees.

Keep cash and bank wallets together. Log income, expenses, and transfers. Track bills, loans, and money owed to you. Set a budget for the month. See a report when you want to know where it went. Travel mode shows another currency while you are away, using the rate already in the app.

Trai is the bookkeeper inside the app. Say “Hey Trai” while WalletTrails is open, or type the sentence. Trai can add an expense, mark a bill, move money between your wallets, or answer “what’s my balance?” It asks when a wallet or an amount is missing. It saves only after you say yes.

A few boundaries, on purpose:
• Voice stays on the phone. The recording is not uploaded.
• Your personal wallets stay personal. A Household sees only the shared ledger you add to it.
• Bank alerts are optional. A message can become a draft you approve. It is not a silent post.
• Trai will not sign you out, take a PIN, or email your books.

WalletTrails is built Pakistan-first, in rupees. The Android app is the product. This page is how we talk about it.
```
