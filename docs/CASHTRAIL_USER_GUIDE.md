# CashTrail — Quick User Guide

**Follow every rupee.**  
CashTrail is a simple money app for Pakistan (PKR). Track wallets, daily spending, bills, loans, and family expenses — all in one place.

---

## Start here

1. Create an account (or sign in).
2. Add your **wallets** (bank + cash) with opening balances.
3. Add your **monthly income** (salary, etc.).
4. Add your **bills** (rent, internet, loans).
5. Use the **+** button every day to log money in or out.

---

## Main tabs

### Home
Your snapshot: total balance, this month’s income & expenses, forecast, and recent activity.

### Wallets
Where your money lives — bank accounts and cash. Create wallets and see each balance.

### Income
Recurring money you expect (salary, clients). Tap **Got paid** when you receive it.

### Bills
Three parts:
- **Monthly costs** — rent, utilities, fixed fees  
- **Money you owe** — loans & installments  
- **Owed to you** — money others should pay you  

Record payments/receipts when they happen.

### Family
Share expenses with spouse or roommates. Create or join a household, add shared spending, and see a fair split.

### Reports
Month-by-month view: expected vs actual, spending by category, wallet history. Export PDF or CSV if needed.

---

## The + button

Tap **+** anytime to add:
- **Expense** — money you spent  
- **Income** — money you received  
- **Transfer** — move money between wallets (not counted as income/expense)

---

## Useful extras

- **Hide amounts** — lock balances with fingerprint, face, or PIN (great in public).  
- **Reminders** — get morning alerts before bills/loans are due.  
- **Offline** — personal entries save on your phone and sync when you’re back online.  
- **Themes** — pick a look you like in Settings.  
- **Support** — message us from Settings if something’s wrong.  
- **Promo / Premium** — redeem a code in Settings if you have one.

---

## First-week tip

Log every expense for 7 days. Then open **Reports** — you’ll clearly see where your rupees go.
