## 1. What Product Hunt is (plain version)

Product Hunt (producthunt.com) is a website where people post new tech products, and a community of early adopters, founders, investors, and journalists check them out every day.

Think of it as a **daily leaderboard for new products**:

1. A product is posted ("launched") for a specific day.
2. Every day starts at **12:00 AM Pacific time** and lasts 24 hours. For you in Pakistan, that is **1:00 PM PKT** (in November–December).
3. During that day, people **upvote** products they like and **comment** with questions or feedback.
4. At the end of the day, the products with the most support are ranked. The top ones get badges like **#1 Product of the Day**, and can later win **Product of the Week / Month**.
5. The launch page stays up forever, so it keeps bringing visitors and reviews after the day ends.

### Who is who on a launch

| Role | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Maker** | Someone who built the product. You are the maker of WalletTrails. |
| **Hunter** | The person who posts the launch. Makers can hunt their own product, which is common now. |
| **Upvoter / commenter** | Product Hunt members who support or discuss the launch. |

### What a launch page contains

- **Name** (max 40 characters) and **tagline** (max 60 characters).
- **Description** (max 500 characters).
- **Thumbnail** (the small square logo) and a **gallery** of images or a video.
- **Topics** (up to 3) and **categories** (up to 3) so people can find it.
- **Pricing type**: free, free with paid options, or paid.
- **The maker's first comment**: your story, why you built it, and what feedback you want. This matters a lot; people read it before upvoting.
- Optional **promo**: a short offer and code for Product Hunt visitors.

### Why bother

- **Early users and honest feedback** from people who like trying new apps.
- **Credibility**: a "Product of the Day" badge on the website and Play Store listing helps.
- **Backlinks and search traffic** to the website.
- **Visibility to press, investors, and partners** who browse the site.

### What Product Hunt does not like

- **Paying for upvotes** or using upvote groups. Product Hunt detects this and can bury or remove the launch.
- **Asking for upvotes directly** ("please upvote!"). Ask people to *check it out and share feedback* instead.
- **Lots of brand-new accounts** voting at once. Votes from fresh accounts count for very little and look suspicious.

## 3. How to build hype before the launch

Start **3–4 weeks before** the launch day.

### 3.1 Warm up your Product Hunt account (start now)

Product Hunt trusts active members more. Every day or two:

- Upvote and leave a **real, helpful comment** on a few launches, especially finance and productivity apps.
- Follow makers in fintech and personal finance.
- Fill in your profile: photo, headline ("Maker of WalletTrails"), and link your LinkedIn and website.

### 3.2 Build a supporter list

Make a simple sheet of people who would genuinely try WalletTrails and are likely to have Product Hunt accounts:

- Your **Google Play closed testers** (you need 12+ anyway; they already know the app).
- Your **LinkedIn network**, friends in tech, university groups.
- Pakistani tech communities (developer, startup, and freelancer groups on WhatsApp, Facebook, Discord, Reddit r/pakistan).
- Existing WalletTrails users (in-app news / push campaigns, which the app already supports).

Ask them a week before: *"I'm launching WalletTrails on Product Hunt on [date]. Can I send you the link that day? Honest feedback in the comments would mean a lot."* Ask those without an account to **create one now**, not on launch day.

### 3.3 Tell the story in public

The strongest angle for WalletTrails is that it is **built for how people in Pakistan and South Asia actually handle money**: cash, several bank wallets, credit cards, installment plans, money lent to friends, and family or trip expenses split across a household. Most finance apps on Product Hunt are built for US bank-sync users; WalletTrails is different.

Content ideas for LinkedIn, Instagram, and X in the weeks before (see `docs/social/instagram-linkedin-facebook.md` for account details):

- "Building in public" posts: why you started WalletTrails, a feature a week.
- Short screen recordings: **Trai** turning "Slanty chips, Rs 50, cash" into an entry; approving a bank SMS; a family trip ledger settling up.
- A teaser 3–5 days before: "Launching on Product Hunt on [date]."

### 3.4 Prepare the website

- Add a banner a few days before: "We're launching on Product Hunt on [date]."
- On launch day, add the official Product Hunt badge linking to the launch page.
- Make sure the **Download** button works and the privacy page is live.

### 3.5 Prepare a launch-day offer

The launch page supports a promo (short text up to 25 characters, code up to 15 characters). WalletTrails already has promo-code redemption in Settings. Example: **"3 months of Plus free"** with code `PRODUCTHUNT`. Only offer this once paid plans actually unlock something.

### 3.6 Prepare the assets

| Asset | Tip |
| --- | --- |
| Thumbnail | The WalletTrails logo, square, readable at small size. |
| Gallery (4–6 images) | First image = the single clearest benefit. Then Home, Trai, Bank alerts, Family, Reports. Add short captions on each image. |
| Video (optional, 30–60 s) | Trai voice entry and bank-SMS approval are the "wow" moments. |
| First comment | Who you are, why you built it, who it is for, what's free, and **one specific question** you want feedback on. |

### 3.7 Launch day plan (Pakistan time)

| Time (PKT) | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| 1:00 PM | Launch goes live. Post your first comment immediately (already drafted). |
| 1:00–2:00 PM | Message your supporter list personally (not a mass blast). Post on LinkedIn, Instagram, X. |
| All day | **Reply to every comment** quickly and warmly. This is the biggest lever you control. |
| Evening (US morning) | Second wave of posts for US and European audiences. |
| Next day 1:00 PM | Day ends. Thank supporters, share the result, add the badge to the website. |

| Ask in chat | What happens |
| --- | --- |
