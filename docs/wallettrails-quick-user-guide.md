# WalletTrails — Quick User Guide

**Follow every rupee.**  
WalletTrails helps you track wallets, credit cards, daily spending, budgets, income, bills, loans, people balances, and shared family expenses — all in one place. PKR is the default currency, and you can choose your home currency during setup or in Settings.

---

## Start here

1. **Create an account or sign in** — use email/password or **Sign in with Google**.
2. **Finish quick setup** — pick your profile type, country, and currency.
3. **Add your wallets** — bank accounts, cash, and credit cards (with opening balance or current card debt and limit).
4. **Add your income sources** — salary, contracts, or one-time work.
5. **Add your bills** — monthly costs, loans you owe, and money owed to you.
6. **Tap `+` anytime** (or ask **Trai**) to log money in or out.

---

## Main tabs

The bottom bar gives you six tabs, plus **Reports** and **Settings** in the top-right corner.

### Home
Your daily snapshot: combined wallet balance (plus card debt if you have credit cards), this month’s income and expenses, expected forecast, quick shortcuts (including **Travel Mode** and **Bank alerts**), and recent activity.

### Wallets
Where your money lives:
- **Bank Wallets** — bank accounts and digital wallets
- **Cash & Wallets** — physical cash
- **Credit cards** — track what you owe, credit limit, available credit, statement day, and due day
- **People** — track money you lend or borrow with someone (either a local person or a linked WalletTrails user who accepts proposals)

### Income
Track money you expect to receive — recurring salary, monthly contracts, one-time projects, or installment work. Tap **Got paid** when money arrives to log it straight into a wallet.

### Bills
Three tabs to stay ahead of due dates:
- **Monthly costs** — rent, utilities, internet, subscriptions
- **Money you owe** — loans and installment plans (tracks paid installments and remaining balance)
- **Owed to you** — client or personal installment plans others owe you

Record payments or receipts directly from each item.

### Budgets
Set an **Overall monthly budget** and per-category limits for each month. See live progress bars as you spend, or tap **Copy last month** to reuse last month’s limits.

### Family
Share expenses with family, roommates, or a trip group without exposing your personal wallets:
- Create a household or join with an invite code
- Run **Ongoing** ledgers (home costs) or **Event** ledgers (trips, weddings, Eid) that you can close when done
- Add shared expenses (paid from your personal wallet, a shared pot, or both)
- Contribute to a shared pot and see equal splits with suggested who-pays-whom settlements
- Export household reports to CSV

### Reports *(top-right chart icon)*
Month-by-month breakdown: expected vs actual income and spending, spending by category, and wallet history (with a wallet filter). Export reports as **PDF** or **CSV**.

---

## The `+` button

Tap the floating **`+`** button anywhere in the tabs to log:
- **Expense** — money you spent (with built-in calculator)
- **Income** — money you received
- **Transfer** — move money between your own wallets (updates balances without inflating income or spending)
- **People** — quickly log lending or borrowing

## Key features

### Trai (in-app assistant)
Tap the **Trai** icon beside the `+` button to type or speak an entry like *"Slanty chips, Rs 50, cash"*, ask *"How was my month?"*, *"What's my balance?"*, or mark a bill paid.
- **Nothing saves without you:** Trai shows a confirmation card first and only saves when you tap **Save** or say *"Yes, save it"*.
- **Voice & "Hey Trai":** On Android, tap the mic to speak, stay in hands-free follow-ups, or say **"Hey Trai"** while the app is open.

### Bank alerts *(Assisted SMS & notification import)*
Open **Bank alerts** from Settings or Home to avoid typing transactions manually:
- Paste or share a bank SMS anytime, or turn on the Android **SMS / notification inbox** to catch alerts automatically.
- Review each detected expense, ATM withdrawal, transfer, income, or reversal and approve or dismiss it.
- WalletTrails remembers your wallet aliases and rules for next time.

### Travel Mode
Going abroad? Turn on **Travel Mode** in Settings:
- Pick a travel currency, fetch the live exchange rate (or set your own), and set trip dates.
- Log expenses in foreign currency while WalletTrails automatically converts and keeps your home-currency books accurate.

### Privacy & Hide amounts
- Tap the **eye icon** anywhere to hide balances and amounts in public.
- Unlock with **fingerprint / Face ID** or a 4–6 digit **WalletTrails Passcode** in Settings.
- Choose auto-lock timing (Immediate, 1 minute, or 5 minutes) and keep exact amounts out of notification text when privacy is on.

### Reminders & Notifications
- Schedule due-date reminders in **Settings → Reminders** (3 days before, 1 day before, and on the due day).
- Choose whether to receive app news and push updates under **Settings → Advanced**.

### Offline support
Personal wallets and personal income, expense, and transfer entries save locally when you are offline and sync automatically when your connection returns (shown in a status banner at the top). Family/household writes require internet so members stay in sync.

### Home-screen widgets *(Android)*
Add the **WalletTrails Balance** widget for your total balance, or the **Trai** widget to jump straight into balance, recent transaction, or monthly questions from your home screen.

### Themes, Categories, Multi-Account & Support
- **Appearance** — switch between **Emerald**, **Ocean**, **Violet**, **Rose**, and **Amber** themes with animated transitions.
- **Categories** — add your own custom expense or income categories in Settings.
- **Switch account** — keep multiple accounts saved on the device and switch between them in Settings.
- **Currency** — change your home currency anytime in Settings.
- **Help Center & Contact Support** — open support threads and follow staff replies right inside the app.
- **Promo / Subscriptions** — redeem promo codes, restore purchases, or manage subscriptions in Settings.

## First-week tip

Log every expense for 7 days (with **`+`**, **Trai**, or **Bank alerts**). Then open **Budgets** and **Reports** — you’ll see right away where your money goes.
